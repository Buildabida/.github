# Code of conduct

We want everyone on our team and in our class to feel safe and welcome in our repos, chats and meetings.

## We do

- Be kind and patient. We're all still learning.
- Give feedback on the work, not the person.
- Give credit for other people's work and data.
- Ask before we share someone's private info.

## We don't

- Insult, harass or make fun of anyone.
- Share private data, keys or passwords.
- Take credit for work we didn't do.

## If something goes wrong

Message Kinah (team lead) or Nadine (org owner) in private. If the problem is about one of them, message our support instructor or mentor instead. We keep reports private, and we act on them.
